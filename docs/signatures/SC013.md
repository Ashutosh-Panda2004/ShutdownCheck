# SC013 — NONZERO_EXIT_CODE

**Stage S7 of the seven stages of correct termination.**

## What was observed

The process exited with a non-zero status after being asked to stop.

## Why it matters

The orchestrator records a crash, which can affect restart accounting.

## How to fix it

Exit cleanly and comfortably inside the grace period, making sure nothing you spawned outlives you.
