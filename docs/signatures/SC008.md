# SC008 — READINESS_FLIP_SLOW

**Stage S2 of the seven stages of correct termination.**

## What was observed

Readiness took too long to start failing.

## Why it matters

Extra seconds of traffic are sent to an instance that is going away.

## How to fix it

Make your readiness endpoint start failing the moment the signal arrives, before anything else. That is what tells the load balancer to stop sending you new work.
