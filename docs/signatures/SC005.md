# SC005 — LISTENER_OPEN_AFTER_WINDOW

**Stage S4 of the seven stages of correct termination.**

## What was observed

The listener kept accepting connections past the de-registration window.

## Why it matters

The service keeps pulling in traffic it cannot finish, so drain never completes.

## How to fix it

Once the de-registration window has elapsed, stop accepting new connections while leaving established ones open.
