# SC010 — SHUTDOWN_BUDGET_EXCEEDED

**Stage S7 of the seven stages of correct termination.**

## What was observed

Shutdown took longer than the declared budget.

## Why it matters

Slow deploys and rollbacks, and a longer window of partial availability.

## How to fix it

Exit cleanly and comfortably inside the grace period, making sure nothing you spawned outlives you.
