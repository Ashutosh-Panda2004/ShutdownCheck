# SC009 — KEEPALIVE_NOT_TERMINATED

**Stage S5 of the seven stages of correct termination.**

## What was observed

Responses after the signal did not ask clients to close the connection.

## Why it matters

Clients keep reusing a socket that is about to die, so the next request fails.

## How to fix it

Tell clients the connection is going away: send `Connection: close` on HTTP/1.1 responses, or a `GOAWAY` frame on HTTP/2, so they stop reusing a socket that is about to die.
