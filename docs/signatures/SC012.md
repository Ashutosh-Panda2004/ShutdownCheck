# SC012 — PORT_HELD_AFTER_EXIT

**Stage S7 of the seven stages of correct termination.**

## What was observed

The port was still accepting connections after the main process exited.

## Why it matters

An orphaned child holds the port, so restarts fail or traffic reaches a zombie.

## How to fix it

Exit cleanly and comfortably inside the grace period, making sure nothing you spawned outlives you.
