# SC001 — SIGTERM_IGNORED

**Stage S1 of the seven stages of correct termination.**

## What was observed

The process showed no reaction to the signal at all.

## Why it matters

Every deploy hard-kills the process and destroys all in-flight work.

## How to fix it

Install a handler for `SIGTERM` and make it start your shutdown sequence. A process with no handler is killed outright, destroying whatever it was doing.
