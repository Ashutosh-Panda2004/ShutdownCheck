# SC015 — ACCEPT_WITHOUT_RESPONSE

**Stage S4 of the seven stages of correct termination.**

## What was observed

Connections were accepted after the signal but never answered.

## Why it matters

Callers hang for their full timeout instead of failing fast.

## How to fix it

Once the de-registration window has elapsed, stop accepting new connections while leaving established ones open.
