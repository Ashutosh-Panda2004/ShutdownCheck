# SC011 — EARLY_EXIT

**Stage S6 of the seven stages of correct termination.**

## What was observed

The process exited while requests were still in flight.

## Why it matters

The service abandons work it accepted, causing silent loss on write paths.

## How to fix it

Wait for in-flight requests to finish before exiting, and flush any background work such as queue consumers or buffered telemetry.
